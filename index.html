MIKE

a fast and compact post-quantum NIKE

MIKE (Module Isogeny Key-Exchange) is a fast and compact post-quantum non-interactive key exchange, based on isogeny assumptions (like the SQIsign signature). MIKE boasts the smallest public (and secret) keys amongst a large range of post-quantum KEMs.

Performance

We provide two conservative parameter sets: rigorous and fast.[1]

Pure C implementation using assembly-optimised finite-field arithmetic; includes no AVX. Implementation is constant time and includes supersingularity verification of public keys. Benchmarked on AMD Ryzen 7 PRO 7840U @ 3.3GHz with turboboost and multithreading disabled. Results rounded to three figures of precision. For comparison, the lattice-based NIKE Swoosh takes 38ms for KeyGen and 2.5ms for SharedKey on the same hardware.
